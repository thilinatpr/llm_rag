                            PART I : SECTION (I) — GENERAL
                                                Government Notifications
NATIONAL PROCUREMENT COMMISSION OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA

 Amendments to the Procurement Guidelines - 2024 on Goods, Works, and Non-Consulting Services Published by
 the National Procurement Commission in terms of Article 156c.(1) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist
                                           Republic of Sri Lanka

THE National Procurement Commission, in the performance of functions assigned thereto under Article 156c.(1) of the
Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, hereby publishes the Amendments to the Procurement
Guidelines - 2024 on Goods, Works, and Non-Consulting Services.

    The following schedule contains amendments that are to be incorporated into the Procurement Guidelines - 2024
on Goods, Works, and Non-Consulting Services Published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 2412/01 on 25.11.2024 to be
effective from 01.01.2025.
      The National Procurement Commission will interpret and clarify any matters related to the said amendments.

			                           By order of the National Procurement Commission,

                                                                                            D. C. Siribaddana,
                                                                                             Secretary General,
                                                                                     National Procurement Commission.
