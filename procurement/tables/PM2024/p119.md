Annexure VIII of Chapter 04
Recommended Time Frame for Completion of Procurement Activities in the Bidding Process with Pre-qualification
(Relevant Reference to the Procurement Guidelines - 4.1.5)

| Value of the Procurement | ICB: Maximum bidding period (Weeks) | ICB: Compulsory bidding period (Weeks) | NCB: Maximum bidding period (Weeks) | NCB: Compulsory bidding period (Weeks) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| More than LKR.2000 Mn. | 17 | 06 | 14 | 03 |
| More than LKR. 750 Mn. to LKR. 2000 Mn. | 15 | 06 | 12 | 03 |
| More than LKR. 250 Mn. to LKR. 750 Mn. | 10 | 06 | 08 | 03 |
| More than LKR. 50 Mn. to LKR. 250 Mn. | 09 | 06 | 06 | 03 |
| Up to LKR. 50 Mn. | 08 | 06 | 04 | 02 |

At the first meeting the PC in consultation with the PE shall determine the procurement time schedule allocating time slots for each action of the relevant procurement strictly adhering to the above time frame. In this regard the PE and PC have the freedom to decide suitable time periods for individual procurement actions (such as bid evaluation, contract award determination, appeal procedure etc.) other than compulsory bidding period depending on the nature and complexity of the procurement.
