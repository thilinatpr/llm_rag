## 2.8 | Payments for Members of Acceptance Committees (adds to Manual 2.8)
PROCUREMENT MANUAL REFERENCE: 2.8. Payments for Members of Procurement Committees, Bid Evaluation Committees, Acceptance Committees, Variation Review Committees and Procurement Appeal Committees, Staff Officers, and Other Officers Assisting in the Procurement Process.

Further to the provisions stipulated under Section 2.8 of the Procurement Manual for Goods, Works and Non-consulting Services – 2024 and the Supplement 01 issued on September 18, 2025 (effective from January 1, 2025), on "Payments for Members of Procurement Committees (PCs), Bid Evaluation Committees (BECs), Staff Officers, and Other Officers Assisting in the Procurement Process", this supplement is applicable for the payment to the Chairman and other Members of the Acceptance Committee (ACs).

The payment schedule below does not apply to Members of Acceptance Committees involved in emergency procurements, or those conducted through the shopping procedure and direct contracting.

The payment schedule below is applicable to Members of Acceptance Committees who conduct Public Procurements with a contract value exceeding or equivalent to LKR Five million (Rs. 5 million) and complete the acceptance procedure within 14 working days.

Schedule of Payments for Members of Acceptance Committees (ACs) (Relevant References to the Procurement Guidelines/Manual – 2.8), Goods, Works or Non-consulting Services. Contract value bands in LKR Million; payments in LKR.

| Contract Value, GOSL Funds (LKR Million) | Contract Value, Foreign Funds (LKR Million) | Payment for Chairman (LKR.) | Payment for other Members (LKR.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| More than or equal to 1000 | More than or equal to 3000 | 12,000 | 10,000 |
| More than or equal to 500 and less than 1000 | More than or equal to 2000 and less than 3000 | 10,000 | 8,000 |
| More than or equal to 250 and less than 500 | More than or equal to 1000 and less than 2000 | 8,000 | 6,000 |
| More than or equal to 100 and less than 250 | More than or equal to 500 and less than 1000 | 6,000 | 4,000 |
| More than or equal to 25 and less than 100 | More than or equal to 250 and less than 500 | 4,000 | 2,500 |
| More than or equal to 5 and less than 25 | More than or equal to 100 and less than 250 | 2,000 | 1,000 |
