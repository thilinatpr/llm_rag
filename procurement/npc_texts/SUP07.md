## 3.1.6 | Direct Contracting: new special circumstance ix, Government Institutions under LKR 2 million (adds to Manual 3.1.6)
Further to the provisions stipulated under Section 3.1.6 of the Procurement Manual for Goods, Works and Non-consulting Services – 2024, concerning "Direct Contracting". The Special Circumstances for applying Direct Contracting for Goods, Works and Non-Consulting Services under Section 3.1.6 of the Procurement Manual -2024 have been amended to include the following as "ix".

"ix. The Direct Contracting Method may be applied for the procurement of goods, works, and non-consulting services from Government Institutions when the contract value is less than Sri Lankan Rupees two million (LKR 2 million). However, the contract awarded under this provision, cannot be subcontracted to third parties."

It is pertinent to note that other provisions of section 3.1.6 remain unchanged.
