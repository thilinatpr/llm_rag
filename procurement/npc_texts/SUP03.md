## 1.4 | Scope of Application: unsolicited proposals not entertained (adds paragraph (d) to Manual 1.4)
In addition to the provisions stipulated under Section 1.4 of the Procurement Manual for Goods, Works and Non-consulting Services - 2024, the following provision shall be added to the content.

Scope of Application (Relevant Reference to the Procurement Guidelines - 1.4)

(d) Unsolicited proposals shall not be entertained in connection with the Procurement Guidelines for Goods, Works, and Non-consulting Services - 2024.
