## | Procurement Guidelines - 2024 for Goods, Works and Non-Consulting Services: coming into force and repeal of the 2006 rules
To All: Secretaries to Ministries, Chief Secretaries of Provincial Councils, Heads of Special Spending Units, Heads of Departments, District Secretaries/Government Agents, Heads of Local Authorities, Heads of State Corporations, Statutory Boards and Government Owned Companies.

The National Procurement Commission (NPC) was established under Article 156B. (1) of Chapter XIX B of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, in May 2023.

02. In terms of Article 156c. (1) of the Constitution, the NPC shall function to formulate fair, equitable, transparent, competitive, and cost effective procedures and guidelines for the procurement of goods and services, works, consultancy services and information systems by government institutions.

03. The NPC published the Procurement Guidelines - 2024 for Goods, Works, and Non-consulting Services in the Extraordinary Gazette No.2412/01, dated 25th of November, 2024 in accordance with Article 156c. (1) of the Constitution. The Procurement Manual - 2024 for Goods, Works, and Non-consulting Services to be used along with the said Procurement Guidelines - 2024 has also been published separately.

04. The provisions of these Guidelines shall come into force with effect from 1st January, 2025.

05. These Guidelines repeal, replace or otherwise supersede the Guidelines and the Manual – 2006 for Goods and Works issued by the National Procurement Agency (NPA) and all the Supplements, Circulars and Directives issued by the NPA, General Treasury and any other Government Institution in the subject of Public Procurement.

06. These Procurement Guidelines have been prepared in association with the major funding agencies to ensure that the Government Procurement Guidelines are harmonized to the maximum extent in order to use for the foreign funded projects as well.

07. These Guidelines constitute a National Policy on Public Procurement and are mandatory and applicable to all public procurements that are carried out by the Government Institutions.

08. Provincial Councils shall also adopt these Guidelines in consultation with the NPC, with necessary amendments to the financial authority levels in conjunction with the delegated financial authorities under the relevant Provincial Council.

09. These Guidelines are issued with the concurrence of the General Treasury.

10. Any clarification/interpretation pertaining to these Guidelines shall be sought from the National Procurement Commission.

D.C. Siribaddana, Secretary General, National Procurement Commission. Copy: Secretary to the President; Secretary to the Prime Minister; Secretary to the Cabinet of Ministers; Secretary to the Treasury; Auditor General.
