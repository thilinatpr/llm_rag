## 2.10.1 | Revised threshold limits: authority of Consultant Procurement Committees for contract award recommendation/determination
REVISED THRESHOLD LIMITS. GUIDELINE REFERENCE: 2.10.1. AUTHORITY OF CONSULTANT PROCUREMENT COMMITTEES FOR CONTRACT AWARD RECOMMENDATION/DETERMINATION.

FIRMS - WHEN COMPETITIVE PROCEDURE (QCBS, QBS, FBS, OR LCS) FOLLOWING GUIDELINE 3.8, 3.9, 3.10 OR 3.11 RESPECTIVELY IS USED

| Authority | Nationally Publicise | Internationally & Nationally Publicise |
|---|---|---|
| Cabinet Appointed Consultant Procurement Committee (CPCC) | Rs. 250 Mn. or more | Rs. 500 Mn. or more |
| Ministry Consultant Procurement Committee (CPCM) | Less than Rs.250 Mn. | Less than Rs. 500 Mn. |
| Department Consultant Procurement Committee (CPCD) or Project Consultant Procurement Committee (CPCP) | Less than Rs. 50 Mn. | Less than Rs. 100 Mn. |

FIRMS - WHEN CQS PROCEDURE (GUIDELINE 3.12) AND SS PROCEDURE (GUIDELINE 3.13) IS FOLLOWED

| Authority | Nationally Publicise | Internationally & Nationally Publicise |
|---|---|---|
| Cabinet Appointed Consultant Procurement Committee (CPCC) | Rs. 50 Mn. or more | Rs. 200 Mn. or more |
| Ministry Consultant Procurement Committee (CPCM) | Less than Rs. 50 Mn. | Less than Rs. 200 Mn. |
| Department Consultant Procurement Committee (CPCD) or Project Consultant Procurement Committee (CPCP) | Less than Rs. 10 Mn. | Less than Rs. 50 Mn. |
| CAO Upon a recommendation made by a committee appointed by CAO | Less than Rs. 1 Mn. | Less than Rs. 10 Mn. |
| AO/PD Upon a recommendation made by a committee appointed by CAO or by delegation | Less than Rs. 500,000 | Less than Rs. 5 Mn. |

INDIVIDUALS (the two columns are the same as above: Nationally Publicise | Internationally & Nationally Publicise; the headers are not repeated on this page)

| Authority | Nationally Publicise | Internationally & Nationally Publicise |
|---|---|---|
| Cabinet Appointed Consultant Procurement Committee (CPCC) | Rs 30 Mn. or more | Rs. 50 Mn. or more |
| Ministry Procurement Committee (CPCM) | Less than Rs. 30 Mn. | Less than Rs. 50 Mn. |
| Department Consultant Procurement Committee (CPCD) / Project Consultant Procurement Committee (CPCP) | Less than Rs. 10 Mn. | Less than Rs. 20 Mn. |
| CAO | Less than Rs. 6 Mn. | Less than Rs.10 Mn. |
| AO/PD | Less than Rs. 2 Mn. | Less than Rs. 6 Mn. |
