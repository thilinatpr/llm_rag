## 1 | Electronic payments to and by State institutions: over-the-counter and online collection, RTGS over Rs. 100 million, refunds of over-collections, bank pre-paid and debit cards (Part II para 1)
1.1 To make it easier for the public to pay the Government electronically for services, State institutions may use (i) over-the-counter payments by electronic means at the institution and (ii) online payments through a website. Guidance on procedure, accounting, reconciliation and payment is on the Department of Public Finance website. The financial institutions that provide electronic collection, and the commission payable, may be decided only with the prior consent of the Department of Public Finance; money for commission, if needed, is arranged through the National Budget Department.
1.2 To reduce delays with cheques, all State institutions may use the Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) system for transactions over Rs. 100 million made through bank accounts.
1.3 Money over-collected because of technical errors or mistakes in electronic collection may be refunded, to speed up refunds, by heads of departments acting as Accounting Officers from the accounted transactions; such refunds need not be submitted to the General Treasury under FR 118.
1.4 The General Treasury has introduced a Bank Pre-paid Card system for payments by State institutions (e-government policy); the maximum limit of a pre-paid card is Rs. 100,000.
1.5 A Bank Debit Card system has been introduced for payments in foreign currency; the maximum limit of a debit card is Rs. 100,000. Guidance on use, reconciliation and accounting is on the Department of Public Finance website.

## 2 | Purchase of air tickets for foreign travel (Part II para 2)
2.1 Officers travelling abroad on official duty shall buy air tickets only from SriLankan Airlines. Travel by another airline may be arranged, with the approval of the Secretary to the Ministry, only where SriLankan does not fly to the destination, where travel time and cost can be reduced, or where SriLankan cannot conveniently provide seats for necessary travel.
2.2 Subject to 2.1, the company/institution that buys the tickets and organises the travel shall be selected under the Procurement Guidelines from among accepted travel agents registered with the Civil Aviation Authority, by calling open quotations, as the one offering the lowest total cost to the Government.
## 3 | General insurance for State institutions (Part II para 3)
3.1 State institutions may obtain general insurance by calling competitive quotations under the procurement guidelines from insurers registered with the Insurance Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (IRCSL) and/or their agents/brokers. Heads of institutions shall consider cost, the nature of the risk covered, the quality of service and convenience to the institution, among other things.
3.2 Details of registered insurers and notices of cancelled registrations are published on the IRCSL website.

## 4 | Communication facilities for Government officers and payment of telephone bills: monthly limits (Part II para 4, table amended by 01/2020 (i) from 01.01.2022)
4.1 Officers' official office telephone bills are paid by the Government up to the limit in column III(a); for official residence telephone, mobile telephone and internet the monthly allowance in column III(b) is paid with the salary.
Current limits (PF Circular 01/2020 (i), 12.01.2022, in force 01.01.2022: telephone expenditure cut by 25% under budget proposal 5.4 for 2022). Maximum monthly expenditure including all taxes and monthly charges, Rs.: (a) official office telephone / (b) official residence telephone, mobile telephone, internet:
- I Secretaries to Ministries, Provincial Chief Secretaries and officers of equal or higher status: 7,500 / 7,500
- II Additional Secretaries to Ministries, Heads of Departments and officers of equal status: 4,500 / 6,000
- III Senior Assistant Secretaries, Directors and Commissioners who are not heads of department, Provincial heads of department, Divisional Secretaries and officers of equal status: 3,000 / 3,750
- IV Deputy Directors, Deputy Commissioners and officers of equal status: 2,250 / 3,000
- V Assistant Directors, Assistant Secretaries, Assistant Commissioners and officers of equal status: 1,875 / 1,875
- VI Administrative Officers: 1,500 / 1,500
- VII Other officers, where the head of department decides a telephone is needed for the service, with the approval of the Secretary to the Ministry: limit for settling telephone bills 1,875
Original 2020 limits (until 31.12.2021): I 10,000 / 10,000; II 6,000 / 8,000; III 4,000 / 5,000; IV 3,000 / 4,000; V 2,500 / 2,500; VI 2,000 / 2,000; VII 2,500.
4.2 International direct dialling, as needed for official work, is given only to Secretaries to Ministries; officers who need it continuously because of the institution's work may be given it with the approval of the Secretary to the Ministry as Chief Accounting Officer.
4.3 No residence/mobile telephone and internet allowance is paid for a period of local or foreign leave, with or without pay, of more than a month.
4.4 An officer acting in, or covering, another post may incur office telephone expenditure for that post, but the allowance in column III(b) is paid for one post only, within its consolidated monthly limit.
4.5 Officers receiving the allowance shall give the head of institution their residence and mobile numbers and a personal e-mail address for the institution's website, and use those facilities adequately for official work.
4.6 Office telephone bills are paid only within the officer's monthly limit; a saving after monthly settlement may be used within the financial year to pay telephone costs above the limit, but may not be carried forward to the next financial year.
4.7 "Equal status" and "higher status" posts are interpreted as in Chapter I of the Establishments Code; problems are referred to the National Pay Commission.

## 5 | Revision of fees charged by State institutions for services to the public, and new fees (Part II para 5, replaced by 01/2020 (III) of 31.05.2024)
PF Circular 01/2020 (III) (31.05.2024) replaces Part II para 05 of 01/2020 and revokes 01/2020 (ii) (20.12.2022), so that State institutions keep fees that reflect at least the cost of the services:
5.1 Institutions empowered by an Act of Parliament or other law to fix, charge or revise fees may revise them as that law provides. New fees and fee revisions so made are notified to the General Treasury (Department of Public Finance) using tables 5.1 and 5.2 of Guideline No. 10.
5.2 (a) Where no Act or other law provides for this, State institutions shall review the fees they charge for services to the public once every three years. (b) If a revision is needed after the review, the Chief Accounting Officer may raise the fee by up to 15%, within a period of not less than three and not more than four years; table 5.1 of Guideline No. 10 is used to notify the Department of Public Finance. (c) If the revenue Accounting Officer decides after the review that no revision is needed, the Chief Accounting Officer informs the Department of Public Finance.
5.3 Except as provided in 5.4, where a State institution decides that a fee must be (a) revised again before three years have passed since the current fee, or (b) revised by more than 15% even once in three years, the revision is sent to the Department of Public Finance, with reasons and the Chief Accounting Officer's recommendation, for the approval of the Secretary to the Treasury/Deputy Secretary to the Treasury, using table 5.3 of Guideline No. 10.
5.4 Even in a case under 5.3, if the revised new fee does not exceed Rs. 1,000, the fee may be revised with the Chief Accounting Officer's approval, disregarding the three-year period and the 15% limit, so that it covers at least the cost.
5.5 A State institution with no legal provision for introducing new fees must obtain the prior approval of the Cabinet of Ministers, through its Ministry, before introducing a new fee; notified using table 5.2 of Guideline No. 10. (The 2020 text said Parliament's approval.)
5.6 A new or revised fee shall be reviewed by a committee of officers with subject knowledge.
5.7 Every new or revised fee under 5.1-5.5 shall be published in the Gazette for public information.
5.8 (a) New and revised fees are notified to the Department of Public Finance (dgpf@pfd.treasury.gov.lk) within 14 days from the date of the Gazette, as in 5.1, 5.2, 5.4 and 5.5. (b) The forms in revised Guideline No. 10 (on the Department of Public Finance website) are used to obtain Treasury approval and to notify revisions. Table 5.1 (revision at institution level): service, year last revised, current fee, new fee, revenue head credited to the Consolidated Fund. Table 5.2 (new fee): service, proposed fee, revenue head, with the reason and basis for each fee. Table 5.3 (Treasury approval under 5.3): service, year last revised, last revised fee, gross revenue collected at the fixed fee in each of the last three years, proposed new fee in rupees and as a percentage, revenue head, with the reason for each revision in a new row or an annexure. If fees go to a fund other than the Consolidated Fund, give the fund and the percentage credited.
Contacts: Director of Public Finance 011-2034508, Assistant Director 011-2484631. Signed A.K. Seneviratne, Secretary to the Treasury (Acting).
Before 31.05.2024 (2020 text): review fees and revise them once in three years by up to 15%, with the approval of the Secretary to the Treasury or a Deputy Secretary on the Ministry Secretary's recommendation; more than 15% or a change of fee structure with the same approval; institutions with legal powers revise under their law; new fees needed Parliament's approval.

## 6 | Fees for urgent services needed by the Government, outside persons and retired officers, and Cabinet-appointed expert committees (Part II para 6, amended by 01/2020 (IV) of 30.08.2024)
6.1 If a Secretary to a Ministry is personally satisfied that no suitable person is available in his Ministry or in Government or semi-Government institutions to provide urgent services needed by the Government, the services of an outside person or group of persons may be obtained. Retired Government officers may also be engaged, except retired officers already serving in a Government institution on re-employment or a temporary basis.
6.2 (a) Such urgent services shall be obtained on a short-term basis not exceeding 90 days, and the most that may be paid to an outside person/group or retired Government officers for such a service is Rs. 150,000. No advance is paid; payment is made after the work is done, with the personal approval of the Secretary to the Ministry.
6.2 (b) (added by 01/2020 (IV), 30.08.2024) An assignment given to an expert committee/officer committee appointed by the Cabinet of Ministers shall be completed within a maximum of 180 days. For the whole assignment, payment may be made at the fee fixed by the Cabinet, or, where the Cabinet has fixed none, up to a maximum of Rs. 100,000 for each chairman/member of the committee. Officers now serving in the State or semi-State service, and retired State or semi-State officers re-employed in a post, are not entitled to this payment.
6.3 The expected Terms of Reference shall be clearly identified and given to the service provider before the service is assigned.
6.4 (as amended by (IV)) Except where the Cabinet fixes the fee and time frame for an expert/officer committee it appoints, where the limits in 6.2(a) and (b) are not sufficient, a request explaining the reasons, with the recommendations of the Accounting Officer and the Chief Accounting Officer, is submitted to the Department of Public Finance for General Treasury approval; the Department submits it to the Deputy Secretary to the Treasury/Secretary to the Treasury for approval. Other provisions of para 6 continue. Signed K.M. Mahinda Siriwardana, Secretary to the Treasury.

## 7 | Consultancy services, postgraduate courses and research by universities and research and development institutes (Part II para 7)
7.1 Universities and R&D institutes earning income from postgraduate courses, research and development work and consultancy services shall: (7.1.1) obtain approval for postgraduate courses through the University Grants Commission and the University Council or Senate; (7.1.2) have research and development work and consultancy services approved by the University Council or Senate; (7.1.3) undertake consultancy services in line with local law and international standards; (7.1.4) a share of 20%-35% of the income from postgraduate courses, R&D and consultancy services may be set aside for the university's infrastructure development; (7.1.5) remit money set aside under 7.1.4 only to university funds established by a law approved by Parliament; (7.1.6) provide R&D and consultancy services without hindering the university's normal work and official duties.
7.2 Guidance on spending this income is on the Department of Public Finance website.

## 8 | Transfer of assets between Government Ministries, Departments, Provincial Councils and statutory institutions (Part II para 8)
8.1.1 The institution transferring assets prepares a complete list, including each asset's book value on the date of transfer.
8.1.2 It submits the list to its Ministry, with copies to the receiving institution and the receiving institution's Ministry.
8.1.3 The two Ministries appoint a committee of persons with expertise to value the assets, and the transfer is made at the committee's valuation.
8.1.4 After the transfer, the receiving institution informs the transferring institution's Ministry in writing, with a copy to the transferring institution, that it has taken over the assets.
8.1.5 Any difference between book value and valuation is adjusted in the transferring institution's accounts after proper approval.
8.1.6 If the assets were received duty-free and the receiving institution is not exempt from duty, the transferring institution recovers the duty and credits it to revenue.

## 9 | Security to be given by Government officers (Part II para 9)
9.1 Audit queries and inspections of the Auditor General show that officers who must give security under FR 880 have not done so; it is emphasised that they must give security under FR 880.
9.2 The requirement and procedure are in FR 880 to FR 893; officers required to give security do so under the Public Officers' Security Ordinance (Chapter 612). Under section (4) of Chapter 612, the Secretary to the Ministry as Chief Accounting Officer may decide the amount and manner of security.
9.3 Under FR 315 (custody and safety of Government money), every head of department as Accounting Officer shall make sure that officers required to give security do so.
## 10 | Reimbursement of annual membership fees of professional bodies (Part II para 10)
10.1 Officers whose scheme of recruitment requires professional qualifications for career development may claim reimbursement of the annual membership fee of one professional body relevant to their profession, legally established in Sri Lanka or abroad; members of several bodies may claim for one body of their choice. The cost is met within the institution's approved annual budget.
10.2 Under section 142 of Chapter XII of the Public Service Commission Procedural Rules (Extraordinary Gazette 1589/30 of 20.02.2009), for officers released on secondment the fee is borne by the budget of the institution to which they are seconded.
10.3 Officers on no-pay leave and/or under disciplinary action are not entitled to the reimbursement.
## 11 | Collecting and recycling waste paper (Part II para 11)
11.1 All State institutions shall collect waste paper and dispose of it to the National Paper Company Ltd (wholly State-owned) after recovering the price fixed by that company.

## 12 | Dates for paying salaries, salary advances and pensions (Part II para 12)
12.1 The fixed dates for paying monthly salaries and salary advances of Government officers, the tri-forces and the teaching service, and pension dates, for the next year are published on the Department of Treasury Operations website on or before 31 October of the current year.
## 13 | Revenue collected under codes for which the Director General of Treasury Operations is the revenue Accounting Officer (Part II para 13)
13.1 Accounting Officers of Ministries, Departments and District Secretariats who collect, administer, supervise and report non-tax revenue under codes for which the Director General of Treasury Operations is the revenue Accounting Officer shall: (13.1.1) collect revenue under FR 142(1)-(4), and Divisional Secretariats also under FR 144; (13.1.2) prepare realistic and accurate revenue estimates and revised estimates under FR 85(1) and (2) and submit them to the Department of Treasury Operations; (13.1.3) submit half-yearly reports on arrears of revenue under FR 128(2); (13.1.4) keep detailed records of revenue refunds under FR 118 for those revenue heads, for the revenue accounts under FR 151. Forms and guidance are on the Department of Treasury Operations website.
