## 5.6.1 | Eligibility for Works Contracts: CIDA grades (amends the CIDA tables of Manual 5.6.1)
Further to the provisions stipulated under Section 5.6.1 of the Procurement Manual for Goods, Works and Non-consulting Services – 2024, relating to "Eligibility for Works Contracts". Demonstration on Equivalent CIDA Registration Grades as Qualification Criteria and CIDA Registration as Equivalent Post Qualification criteria under section 5.6.1 of Procurement Manual - 2024 are amended as follows, it is pertinent to note that other provisions of section 5.6.1 are remain unchanged.

Following directive on the selection of CIDA-registered Construction Contractors:

| Estimated Value of Work to be performed under the Contract (X) (excluding VAT & other taxes) – LKR Million | CIDA Grade/s |
|---|---|
| 4.0 ≥ X | C9, C8 and C7 |
| 10.0 ≥ X > 4.0 | C8, C7 and C6 |
| 20.0 ≥ X > 10.0 | C7, C6 and C5 |
| 50.0 ≥ X > 20.0 | C6, C5 and C4 |
| 100.0 ≥ X > 50.0 | C5, C4 and C3 |
| 300.0 ≥ X > 100.0 | C4 and above |
| 600.0 ≥ X > 300.0 | C3 and above |
| 1200.0 ≥ X > 600.0 | C2 and above |
| 3000.0 ≥ X > 1200.0 | C1 and above |
| 6000.0 ≥ X > 3000.0 | CS1 and above |
| X > 6000.0 | CS2 |
