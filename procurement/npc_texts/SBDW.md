## 45 | Change in Law
Summary of clause 45 (Change in Law) of the ICTAD Works standard conditions. Check clause 45.1 and its definition of "Change in Law" in the document and in your contract.
- The Engineer adjusts the Contract Price for a change in law that happens in the period from 28 days before the bid closing date until the Certificate of Completion is issued (or the contract is terminated under clause 59).
- What is adjusted is the change in the tax, duties and other levies the contractor has to pay. There is no adjustment where the change is already reflected in the Contract Price, or where it results from the price adjustment clause 47.1 or otherwise.
- "Change in Law" means a new law, or a change to, repeal of or modification of an existing law of the country (including regulations and directives made under it), or a change in how a competent court interprets or applies a law compared with before the date of the agreement. It counts only if it relates to taxation, to rationing or prohibiting an activity, or to duties and import/export levies; is beyond the contractor's control; and materially affects the contractor's performance of its responsibilities under the contract.

## 47 | Price Adjustment
Summary of clause 47 (Price Adjustment) of the ICTAD Works standard conditions. Check clause 47 and the Contract Data entry for clause 47 in the document and in your contract.
- Unless the Contract Data says otherwise, a formula adjusts the payments for rises or falls in the cost of labour, materials, plant and other inputs to the Works; the result is added to or deducted from the payment to the contractor.
- The clause says the same formula also deals with changes in legislation, not only in costs. In words, the formula is: 0.966 times (the current valuation minus the value of non-adjustable work), divided by 100, times the sum over the inputs of (input percentage x (current index minus base index) / base index).
- Input percentages are each input's share of the construction cost at the prices one month before the bid was submitted, and are listed in the Contract Data. The indices are those published by ICTAD.
- The price is not altered in any other way for movements in input prices, even if special circumstances make the contractor pay more.
