## 2.8 | Payments for Members of Variation Review Committees and Procurement Appeal Committees (adds to Manual 2.8)
PROCUREMENT MANUAL REFERENCE: 2.8. Payments for Members of Procurement Committees, Bid Evaluation Committees, Acceptance Committees, Variation Review Committees and Procurement Appeal Committees, Staff Officers, and Other Officers Assisting in the Procurement Process.

Further to the provisions stipulated under Section 2.8 of the Procurement Manual for Goods, Works and Non-consulting Services – 2024 on "Payments for Members of Procurement Committees (PCs), Bid Evaluation Committees (BECs), and Staff Officers, and Other Officers Assisting in the Procurement Process", the applicable payments for the Chairman and Members of the Variation Review Committees (VRCs) and Procurement Appeal Committees (PACs) are provided below.

Schedule of Payments for Members of Variation Review Committees (VRCs) (Relevant References to the Procurement Guidelines/Manual – 2.8 and 9.2). The variation value bands are in LKR Million; the payments are in LKR.

| Net Aggregate Variation Value (In LKR. Million) | Payment for Chairman (LKR.) | Payment for other Members (LKR.) |
|---|---|---|
| More than 1000 | 17,500 | 12,500 |
| More than or equal to 500 and less than 1000 | 12,500 | 10,000 |
| More than or equal to 250 and less than 500 | 10,000 | 7,500 |
| More than or equal to 100 and less than 250 | 7,500 | 5,000 |
| More than or equal to 25 and less than 100 | 5,000 | 4,000 |
| More than or equal to 5 and less than 25 | 2,500 | 1,500 |
| More than or equal to 2 and less than 5 | 1,250 | 1,000 |

Payments for Members of Procurement Appeal Committees (MPAC, DPAC, PPAC, RPAC) (Relevant References to the Procurement Guidelines/Manual – 2.8 and 8.5.7). Payments in LKR.

| Relevant Procurement Appeal Committee | Payment for Chairman (LKR.) | Payment for other Members (LKR.) |
|---|---|---|
| MPAC | 7,500 | 5,500 |
| DPAC | 3,500 | 2,500 |
| PPAC | 3,500 | 2,500 |
| RPAC | 1,750 | 1,250 |
