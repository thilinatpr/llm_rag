## 1.5 | Annexure III of Chapter 01: Non-collusion Declaration (replaces the earlier Annexure III, Non-collusion Affidavit)
Annexure III of Chapter 01 is hereby replaced with the following.

Annexure III of Chapter 01 - Non-collusion Declaration (Relevant Reference to the Procurement Guidelines - 1.5)

I, the undersigned bidder/ bidder's representative/ bidder's agent, honestly, truthfully and solemnly declare that;
(a) I, nor any other member, agent or representative of the firm/ company/ corporation/ partnership/ sole proprietorship that I represent, have entered into any combination, collusion or similar agreement with any person in connection with the prices to be submitted by any person with respect to the invitation for bid;
(b) I, nor any person who represents me have acted to prevent any person from submitting a bid or to induce any person to refrain from submitting a bid in connection with the intention for bid (Bid No. ……….);
(c) This bid is not submitted in collusion with any other bid and is not made pursuant to any agreement, understanding or association with any other person in relation to such bid.

I declare that, I have not received and will not accept any discount, fee, reward, commission or anything of value, directly or indirectly, from any person, company or corporation in connection with the submission of this bid.
I further declare that, I have not given and will not give any discount, fee, reward, commission or anything of value, directly or indirectly, to any person, company or corporation in connection with the submission of this bid.
I, taking full responsibility for ensuring the absence of collusion, hereby pledge to abide by fair and ethical competitive practices throughout the entire procurement process and to fully comply with the relevant Procurement Guidelines issued by the National Procurement Commission.
I hereby declare that all the statements made by me above are true and correct.
……………………………………… Signature of the Declarant

## 8.2 | Communication to All Bidders on the Intention to Award of the Contract (replaces Manual 8.2)
Section 8.2 is hereby replaced with the following.

Communication to All Bidders on the Intention to Award of the Contract (Relevant Reference to the Procurement Guidelines - 8.2)

Intention to award of the contract shall be issued and communicated to all bidders in writing, within Seven (07) calendar days, after the determination of the High Level Procurement Committee, Standing High Level Procurement Committee, Ministry Procurement Committee, Departmental Procurement Committee, Project Procurement Committee or Regional Procurement Committee.

In the event of an appeal, after the appeal process, the approval for the award of contract shall be obtained from the relevant approving authority. The relevant approving authority is spelt out in Section 2.9.(B) of the Manual.
