# Social Security Contribution Levy Act, No. 25 of 2022: IRD consolidated text, amendments up to 9 April 2026 (reviewed 2026-11-09)

Source: https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Acts_SSCL/SSCL_Cons_Act_-_2026_Changes.pdf (46 pages, Word-made, text layer; "prepared by
the Tax Policy & Legislation Unit, Inland Revenue Department", May 26, 2026). Its own words: "includes all amendments made up to April 09,
2026 ... This is not a statutory consolidation, but rather a convenient reference." Incorporates Acts No. 15 of 2023, 15 of 2024, 24 of 2025
and 10 of 2026. (Its first body page still says "Incorporating the Amendments up to on March 20, 2024"; the cover and the list of
amending Acts say April 9, 2026.)

Made with billrag/consolidation.py: body and margin split by position (lines, not blocks), section numbers only in sequence and only
before the First Schedule, rows by vertical overlap, IRD's change notes kept inline at the end of the paragraph they stand beside:
"[IRD note: Sec 2(1) of 10 of 2026]". 22 change notes. Parses to sections 1-61 (the table of contents lists 1-61) and both Schedules.

Checked section by section against the certified Act (documents.gov.lk 25/2022, parsed the same way), ignoring margin titles:
- Differ as expected (IRD notes on them): s.4 (registration thresholds and dates: before 1 Jan 2024; 1 Jan 2024 to 1 Jul 2026; from
  1 Jul 2026) and s.5 (cancellation thresholds, same periods).
- Spelling or spacing only: s.3 "sub paragraphs", s.28 "sufficient" (certified Act misprints "sufficiant"), s.32/s.40 "office bearer",
  s.53 "go down", s.60 "25F".
- Every other section matches word for word. The Schedules were not compared (the amendments rewrite them; the IRD notes say where).

Corrected here (IRD's text against the certified Act, no amendment touches them):
- s.58: the tenth paragraph is printed "(g)" a second time -> (j), as in the certified Act (a wrong label would give wrong citations).
- s.56: "data essages" -> "data messages".

Extraction faults found and fixed during the review (each checked again afterwards): the last body line of a page (y 750-755) was cut by a
footer filter (s.18, s.29, s.30, s.51, s.54, s.59 lost a line); "18." ending a sentence was taken for a paragraph label; a bold section
number set lower than its text; margin years ("2026") dropped as page numbers.
Not checked: the Schedules item by item against the amending Acts. Waiting for the owner's acceptance ("accepted_by").
